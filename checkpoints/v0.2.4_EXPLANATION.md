# v0.2.4 — What happened, in plain language

At v0.1.5 the shop was open but stood alone. Each phone was its own separate storefront, and the outgoing-mail tray kept filling with copies for a head office that didn't exist yet. That head office now exists on AWS, drawn up from one set of architectural blueprints (the SAM stack). Atomic got its own badge reader (a Cognito app client) at Forge's badge office, so the badge you already use for Forge opens Atomic's door too. Past the bouncer at the front desk (API Gateway with the Cognito check) sits a data clerk who is called in only when there's work (a Lambda function). The clerk files everything into a filing cabinet with one drawer per person (DynamoDB). When a memo arrives, the clerk checks its date stamp and ignores it if the drawer already holds a newer version, so an old phone can't overwrite fresh work. When a phone asks for updates, it only asks for the pages filed since its last visit. Behind the scenes, the accountant sends a warning note if the month's bill heads past $5, a camera with a pager emails you if the clerk keeps failing, the cabinet is photocopied so it can be rewound, and a queue rope at the front desk stops a runaway loop from running up the bill. In the shop itself, the counter learned to total expense lists, and the lobby got a pink paint option.

This last round was about the everyday things that didn't work the way you expected. Before, the date box in the editor was the phone's own control, and on some phones it simply ignored taps inside the pop-up. Now Atomic draws its own small month calendar, which behaves the same on every phone, and the same calendar lets you move any task to any day, not just tomorrow. Before, "buy groceries on Friday" turned into a chore that came back every week. Now the receptionist listens to how you say it: "Friday" means the coming Friday, once, and "Fridays" or "every Friday" means every week.

The doubled categories came from two phones each setting up their own starter folders before they were synced. The filing cabinet saw two different "Fitness" folders and kept both. Now the starter folders carry the same label on every phone, so they line up when synced. A clean-up also runs every time the shop opens or the mail arrives: folders with the same name are combined into the oldest one, their contents are moved across, and the spare is retired. That retirement goes out through the outgoing-mail tray too, so the filing cabinet and your other phones get tidied as well.

Two new spaces were added. The NOTES tab is a shelf for things that don't belong to a particular day, like books to read, recipes, quotes and reminders to yourself. Any note can be pinned so it also appears at the bottom of Today every day, where its checklist can be ticked and added to. The ONGOING section holds goals without a deadline. They sit on Today every day until you finish them, they never count as "missed", and once done they can either step aside or stay in view, whichever you prefer. Both are kept as ordinary items, so they already travel to the filing cabinet and to your other phones without any change to the head office.

What's still ahead: the private PA channel that will let Forge and Atomic tell each other when a shared fitness goal is done, and the receptionist's alarm clock plus postcards for reminders.
