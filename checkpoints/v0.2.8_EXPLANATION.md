# v0.2.8 — What happened, in plain language

In v0.2.7, tapping a list item opened a box with the item's link, but adding or changing the link still opened a separate sheet that slid up from the bottom of the screen. That meant the box held the link, while the typing happened somewhere else. This version moves the typing into the box. Tapping "+ ADD A LINK" (or "CHANGE LINK") turns the box's contents into a text field with SAVE, CANCEL and, when a link exists, REMOVE. You type or paste the address right where the link will appear, press SAVE or Enter, and the box goes back to showing the OPEN button. A bad address shows its error message under the field instead of in a pop-up.

A few details keep this from being annoying. The box remembers that you are typing and what you typed, so if the screen repaints while you are in the middle of it (for example, a sync from your partner's phone arrives), the field stays open with your text still in it. Tapping another item, or the same item again, closes the box and throws away anything unsaved, which is the same as pressing CANCEL. Saving, removing and undoing work as before: the link is one field on the item, undo brings it back for five seconds, and on a shared list the change goes to the other person like any other edit.

Nothing changed in the cloud or in AWS. The only files touched are the screen code (`ui.js` and `app.js`) and the stylesheet. The old pop-up for a whole list's link under the list's ⋯ menu is still there, because that link belongs to the list, not to one item. The two browser tests were updated to type into the box and to check that no pop-up appears.
