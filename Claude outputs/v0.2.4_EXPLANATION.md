# v0.2.4 — Dates that work, smarter day names, notes and ongoing goals

## What changed, in one breath
Editing a task's date now works on the phone, "on Friday" means *this coming Friday* while "Fridays" means *every Friday*, tasks can be moved to any day from a calendar, duplicate categories are merged and can't come back, there's a new NOTES screen for things that don't belong to a day, and a new ONGOING section holds goals that stay on Today until you finish them.

## 1. The date field that ignored taps
**Before:** the editor used the phone's built-in date box (`<input type="date">`). That box is drawn by the phone's browser, not by the app, and on some phones it simply doesn't react when it sits inside a pop-up sheet (`<dialog>`), so tapping it did nothing.

**After:** Atomic draws its own date picker (`public/js/views/datepick.js`). The field shows the chosen day in words ("Friday, October 9 · in 6 days"). Tapping it unfolds a month grid with TODAY, TOMORROW, IN A WEEK and NO DATE shortcuts. Because the grid is made of ordinary buttons, it responds to taps the same way every other button in the app does. The chosen day is kept in a hidden form field, so the save code didn't need to change.

**Concept: native controls vs. your own.** Native controls are free and accessible but look and behave differently on every phone. Building your own costs a little code but behaves the same everywhere — worth it when the native one is broken in your layout.

## 2. "Friday" vs. "Fridays"
**Before:** any day name made a weekly habit, so "buy groceries on Friday" repeated forever.

**After:** the typing box's parser (`parseDayWords` in `model.js`) now looks at *how* you wrote the day:
- one plain day — `on friday`, `fri`, `this friday` → a one-time task due the nearest Friday (today, if today is Friday)
- `next friday` → the Friday after today
- plural or "every" — `fridays`, `every friday`, `weekdays` → repeats every week
- two or more days — `mon wed fri` → repeats (a single task can't be due on three days)

Time blocks follow the same rule: `dentist 2pm-3pm thursday` is one appointment, `work 9am-5pm fridays` repeats. The rules are listed under the `?` button.

**Concept: parsing.** A parser turns loose human text into structured data (`{ kind: 'task', dueDate: '2026-10-09' }`). The new helper `nextWeekday(today, 5)` counts forward to the next Friday.

## 3. Move a task to any day
The ⋯ menu on a task now has **Move to another day…**, which opens the same month grid in its own sheet. Tapping a day sets the task's due date (`state.moveTo`). Undo works as usual.

## 4. Duplicate categories
**Why it happened:** each device created its own starter categories with random ids. When two devices synced, the cloud saw two different "Fitness" records and kept both.

**The fix, in two parts:**
- *Clean-up:* `planDedupe` (in `model.js`) groups categories by name, ignoring capitals and spaces. In each group it keeps the oldest one, moves every item and subcategory onto it, and marks the extras deleted. Every device picks the same keeper (oldest, then smallest id), and the deletions are synced, so the cloud and your other devices end up clean too. It runs when the app opens, after every sync and after importing a backup.
- *Prevention:* starter categories now get fixed ids (`el_seed_fitness`), so two fresh devices create the *same* record instead of twins, and adding a category or subcategory whose name already exists reuses the existing one.

**Concept: idempotent.** Running the clean-up a second time changes nothing — a property you want for anything that runs automatically and repeatedly.

## 5 & 6. NOTES screen, pinned to Today
A new **NOTES** tab holds things that don't belong to a day: books to read, recipes, quotes, reminders. Type `books to read: dune, piranesi` to make a checklist note, or use **+ NOTE WITH TEXT** for free writing. Typing the same title again adds to that note. Tap **☆ TODAY** and the note also appears in a NOTES section at the bottom of Today every day, where you can tick items and add new ones. From Today's typing box, `#note title: items` works too.

**How it's stored:** a note is an ordinary item with `kind: 'note'`, a checklist (`items`), text (`note`) and `pinned`. Because it reuses the existing item records, it already saves offline, syncs to the cloud and works with search, undo and backups — no database or back-end change was needed.

## 7 & 8. ONGOING goals
Add a task with `#ongoing`, `#goal`, `#someday` or `#anytime`, choose **ONGOING** under WHEN in the editor, or pick **Make it ongoing** from the ⋯ menu. It sits in a new foldable **ONGOING** section every day until you check it. It isn't counted as "missed" on days you don't finish it, and "move unfinished to tomorrow" leaves it alone. Once checked, it stays crossed out that day. If you want finished goals to stay in view, tap **SHOW N FINISHED** at the bottom of the section (tap **HIDE FINISHED ONES** to put them away). The choice is remembered.

**How it's stored:** an ongoing goal is a task with `ongoing: true` and no due date. The visibility rule (`showsOn` in `model.js`) shows it on today and later days while it's open, and only on the day it was finished once it's done, unless "show finished" is on.

## Two small fixes found along the way
- Opening a note or habit from search sometimes showed an empty sheet. Closing one sheet wiped the sheet that had just opened. Now the wipe only happens if no new sheet has opened.
- A sheet's tap handler could carry over into the next sheet. `openSheet()` now clears it.
